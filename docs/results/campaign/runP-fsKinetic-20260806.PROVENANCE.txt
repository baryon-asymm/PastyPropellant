WHAT THE NEW CLOSURE IS
    f_s(p) = 1 / (1 + a0 + a1 * w_fine * (p/p_ref)^m)
    a0 = 0.9966, a1 = 3.3653, m = 0.71, p_ref = 1 MPa
Coverage as the steady state of a rate competition: the pocket surface gains skeleton
where condensed residue accumulates and loses it where the carbonaceous binder residue
holding the skeleton is oxidised away.  Both rates written per unit skeleton area, so
the layer thickness delta CANCELS IDENTICALLY - this closure cannot inherit the
delta -> 0 non-identifiability, and it is independent of skeletonContactFactor.
    a0                    burnout by the binder's own decomposition products.  The
                          binder is itself a perchlorate (XPEPA-22E), so this channel
                          exists with no AP in the pocket; it carries no pressure term.
    a1 * w_fine * p^m     burnout by the FINE AP inside the pocket (the coarse fraction
                          is the pocket wall, not pocket material).

WHY THIS SHAPE AND NOT THE INVENTORY.  The measured coverage of Bas_4 (0.529 at 1 MPa)
EXCEEDS its whole condensed inventory phi_Al + phi_C = 0.386, so coverage cannot be
inventory-limited.  Normalising K by phi_C or phi_C*phi_s was tried and is worse:
leave-one-composition-out error rises 13..21 % -> 19..42 % and a1 turns negative.
And no law of the form K ~ p^a / r^b can work at all: Bas_1 and Bas_4 share nu = 0.70
yet need d lnK/d lnp of 0.609 and 0.015, so the pocket recipe must enter.

CALIBRATION, AND WHAT IT IS NOT.  Three constants, fitted on Bas_2/Bas_3/Bas_4 - one
binder, the same 0.5 % activated carbon, differing only in AP dispersity, so every
input is published (Babuk et al., 10th Int. Seminar on Flame Structure 2024, Table 1).
In-sample 4.7..8.6 % RMS on f_s; leave-one-composition-out 12.7..20.9 %.  This is NOT
a zero-fit closure the way run M's is.  The claim is 3 shared constants carrying a
transferable recipe dependence, in place of 12 per-composition coefficients that
transfer to nothing.

WHAT THE TARGET ACTUALLY IS.  The shipped polynomial was PROVEN this session to be
Babuk's measured agglomeration curve: agglomeration_coefficients evaluated at the
points of his Fig. 2 reproduce them to <= 0.023 over 20 points, inside the plotted
confidence intervals, for all five compositions.  f_s_poly = Z_m^a(p) / Z_pocket is
his stated correlation.  So "beating the polynomial" means describing a measurement
with fewer and more transferable numbers, not beating an arbitrary fit.
