WHAT THE NEW CLOSURE IS
    f_s(p, T_s) = n_C(s)(T_pore, p) / n_C,total ,  T_pore = (T_flame,skeleton + T_s)/2
The skeleton is the carbonaceous residue of binder pyrolysis; how much survives is
decided by equilibrium at the pocket conditions, not by a fit.  Numerator: Gibbs
minimisation over the pocket matrix (binder + Al + FINE AP - coarse AP and HMX are
the pocket walls, not pocket material).  Denominator: the carbon that matrix holds.
Both from data/propellants.01234.json + data/propellant_components.json and the
AerospacePropellantThermodynamics submodule.

ZERO FITTED NUMBERS.  This replaces 22 polynomial coefficients that were byte-
identical to the agglomeration measurements.  Nothing in the new closure is
calibrated against those measurements or against anything else.

WHY PRESSURE LOWERS IT.  C(s) + 2H2 -> CH4 is the only carbon sink here with
dn < 0, so the only one pressure drives forward.  Boudouard (C + CO2 -> 2CO) and
steam gasification (C + H2O -> CO + H2) go with dn = +1 and are suppressed by
pressure.  Six transport candidates for the same trend all gave the WRONG sign,
because any length of the form (transport coefficient)/(velocity) shrinks with p.

IMPLEMENTATION.  f_s now depends on T_s, so it is evaluated INSIDE the surface-
temperature bisection instead of once at matrix-build time.  Loop gain measured on
the shipped table: |d ln f_s / dT_s| <= 0.16 %/K, gentle enough not to threaten the
bracketing.  The Gibbs minimisation itself does not depend on the search vector, so
it is tabulated ahead of the run into data/skeleton_carbon_equilibrium.json by
generate_skeleton_carbon_equilibrium.py (250 equilibria, 1224 s).
